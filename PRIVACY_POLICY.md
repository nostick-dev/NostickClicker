# Privacy Policy — NostickClicker
Effective: 2026 · Owner: Nostick

**Short version: NostickClicker collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- **No internet.** The app does not request the Internet permission and never
  connects to any server.
- **No data collection.** We do not collect, store, share or sell personal data,
  device identifiers, screen content, keystrokes, contacts, messages, files,
  location, camera or microphone data.
- **Accessibility service.** Used only to perform the taps and swipes you set up.
  The app does not read, record or process what is shown on the screen
  (`canRetrieveWindowContent` is disabled).
- **Overlay permission.** Used only to draw the floating menu, click points and
  on-screen controls.
- **Local settings.** Click speed and your control layouts are stored only on your
  device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Optional Shizuku mode.** If you enable it, touches are sent to Android through
  the Shizuku app on your device. Nothing leaves your device.
